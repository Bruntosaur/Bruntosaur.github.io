Assignment 02 — Woodpecker II

The Peckoning

Notes

The long awaited sequal of the notorious triple-A(vian) favourite is finally here. With new remastered graphics, you get to experience what it's like to be a woodpecker!

You control the beak of the woodpecker with your own beak. Line yourself up with the targets and click to peck. Ensure proper distance from the screen, half a meter should do fine. If you're in possesion of a wireless mouse you can play standing further away from the screen, using your entire body to align yourself.

The creator of the soothing background score can be found here.
